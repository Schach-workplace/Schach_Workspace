# Planning and observed goals

Calendar and workload use bounded complete authorized inputs and company civil dates. Weekly exposure is recorded weekly capacity, not hours worked or a productivity score. Timed commitments keep their UTC instant/IANA zone; DST gaps are rejected and repeated times require a deliberate offset.

Goal metadata and append-only observations are separate. A person records a value, effective instant, reason and reviewed authorized evidence; task completion never fabricates a business outcome. Corrections append history and do not replace a newer current observation. Legacy current values remain qualified, with coverage boundaries rather than invented observations. Owner handover does not transfer personal reminder preferences.

After a version conflict, discard the submitted intent deliberately and load the latest goal/value/preference and version through the current form. A transport-unknown result retries the same key, target, observation, evidence hashes and versions; refresh never silently rebases it. Current-access checks withhold fetched evidence/candidates while same-principal authored controls can stay in RAM. Confirmed source/session/company loss clears affected recovery.

Structured records remain separate from task/expense approvals. Choose current source readers, search the eligible audience and optionally set a response deadline in the company timezone. A decision needs an independent non-viewer source reader. A blank deadline stays blank. Unknown structured creation keeps its entire submitted body/UTC deadline for retry. The server always rechecks source revision/history and authority.
