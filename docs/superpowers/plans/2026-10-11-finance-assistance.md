## Review focus

1. Actual source parent/department changes without membership-basis change during OCR or before replay: current source permission is required at every boundary, output hidden (Tasks2–4,8–9).
2. Commit succeeds but ACK/invalidation fails: same ID/code/receipt and no second write, including source==target parent advanced by acceptance itself (Tasks1,4,8).
3. Scanner fails/source expires/cancels/old worker finishes after lease reclaim: no provider input or stale resurrection; retained destination evidence survives scratch cleanup (Tasks2–4,9).
4. Identical statement-valued rows/explicit credits/ambiguous locale mapping/totals above JS safe range: distinct occurrence IDs, exact totals, no guessing/payment/partial allocation (Tasks1,6–8).
5. Camera return/background/keyboard interruption/late old-company response: current authorization and scoped recovery preserve inspected human intent; CTA reachable (Tasks8–9).
