## Global constraints

- “Company rank does not confer private conversation access.” Leadership may see ordinary authorized company work; it cannot read, transfer or publish private messages by rank.
- “Finance retains exact amounts, independent review, receipt exceptions and recorded overrides.” Preserve expense creator, approver, payer, receipt uploader and finance-event actors. No handover writes any approval, payment, amount or banking fact.
- “Task changes retain original deadlines, evidence requirements, independent acceptance and optimistic versions.” No bulk assignment SQL bypasses the task engine. Accepted task ownership/reviewer is frozen history; no departure-driven reopening.
- “Keep Go/GORM/Encore/PostgreSQL and React/Expo.” “Do not introduce a second task engine, unrestricted global search, autonomous AI approvals or a microservice fleet.” “India remains the deployment region.”
- “Idempotent action keys survive transport retries.” Current authority/source/result checks precede canonical replay; replay never reapplies a later current state or duplicates tasks, audits, facts, reminders or provider revocations.
- “Touch targets of at least 44 logical pixels,” usable “320–430px layouts,” keyboard/safe-area support, accessible labels/status, large text and deliberate destructive confirmation on web/native.
- Use human eligible pickers, not typed technical IDs. Fetched previews/details are withheld during reauthorization; transient network errors retain scoped authored RAM input/frozen attempts. **Binding common lifecycle, future implementation:** graph-only readBasis/Company.PermissionEpoch invalidation preserves only qualifying submitted unknown canonical requests with exact frozen key/target/dependencies/body/CAS and unchanged positive actor authority basis, while ordinary drafts/captures and fetched projections still clear. Actor/session/member-access or actual source/interval loss, switch/logout/confirmed revocation clears the relevant attempts/secrets immediately and fences delayed callbacks. No passwords, bearer/cookie tokens, session hashes, push tokens, private text or credential bindings in logs/read DTOs. The authorized onboarding apply request alone carries transient new-account password; it is never echoed or logged.
- A permission epoch is invalidation metadata, never proof of current source authority. Company membership graph changes affect managers/department scope as well as the changed person. Every read, apply, replay and queued worker still reloads actual authority.
- Company departure does not suspend/delete the global User or alter other memberships. Account sessions are currently global; relevant reauthentication may affect another company and must be explained before apply. Global offboard remains explicit platform-admin-only and preserves its stronger all-company suspension semantics.
- Guided departure requires complete reviewed source handover and has no force flag or silent direct-revocation fallback. Existing authorized active=false and platform globalOffboard preserve their role/rank/nonself/founder/owned-unarchived-conversation guards, including the current global-owned-DM distinction, but gain no live-task/private-review/all-duty inventory veto. No private close/reviewer-transfer API is introduced, and source creator/reviewer authority remains unchanged.
- No automatic private invitations. Reactivated membership does not restore old private intervals, private derived outputs or old source-bound drafts; explicit owner reinvitation starts at the next sequence with a fresh conversation epoch.
- Root8080 and the user's database remain intact. Verification uses controller-owned fictional fixtures and isolated runtime ports. DOM/export/emulator evidence never substitutes for physical keyboard, screen-reader, background push/call, iOS or organization signing evidence.

## Review focus

1. Concurrent administrators change different reporting links or leave companies while a user signs in: no deadlocks, post-revocation old session validity or cycles; guided departure has no untransferred mandatory duty or partial transfer, while direct revocation truthfully retains unresolved duties (Tasks 1, 4, 5).
2. A reviewed membership is unchanged but its manager graph, source template or assigned task changes: the captured company graph and exact source versions become stale for an uncommitted first write, requiring a new deliberate preview rather than silent rebasing. An already-submitted unknown canonical apply retains its original request under the binding lifecycle and resolves through current-authorized canonical lookup before historical first-write guards (Tasks 2–5).
3. An accepted task or financial history references the departing person: keep that history unchanged while transferring only live work, without forced reopen, waived review or manufactured payment actors (Tasks 4–5).
4. An administrator lacks private ownership/title/roster access: return an opaque unresolved requirement, perform no private transfer, and roll back every guided handover if the requirement remains; the distinct direct-revocation action remains available under existing guards without a private-review veto or disclosure (Tasks 1, 4–7).
5. A successful apply loses its response, then the person/session/source changes: recover one canonical result only under current authorization, retain the exact frozen attempt on transport failure and qualifying graph-only refresh, and never restore departed private content or repeat invalidation. A graph change never implies own success; true actor/source loss hard-clears and blocks late ACK disclosure (Tasks 1–7).
